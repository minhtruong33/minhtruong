Coach.Win

Coach.Win elevates strategy and planning with experienced business builders, AI-powered tools, and a forward-thinking process that isn't constrained by a generic business operating system. It's more than facilitation — it's your strategic advantage. The company is owned by John Lund and Rob Simons.

As an AI Operations Analyst, I design and ship the firm's AI systems:

  • Architect and deploy AI agents across multiple surfaces — scheduled tasks in Claude Cowork, Microsoft 365 Cowork, and schedulers built into the firm's own applications.
  • Design reusable brand voice and visual identity skills, so clients produce on-brand deliverables without rebuilding branded documents from scratch.
  • Build an intelligent document processing pipeline — Custom GPT, n8n, and Excel — that turns raw photos and field data into tank inspection reports, replacing a multi-hour manual process.
  • Build the two products below: the GTM Engine and Value Compass.

GTM Engine

The Coach.Win go-to-market command center — a strategy hub plus scheduled AI agents that qualify inbound prospects for weekly outreach, monitor competitors and market developments, surface target accounts from trade show listings with tailored talking points, and draft LinkedIn posts, blog articles, and newsletters from uploaded documents. All of it runs on your own SharePoint.

Value Compass

A web app where users enter their corporate initiatives and business pain points, then get AI-driven insights linking them to the underlying value drivers and their strategic alignment — mapped to Andrew Reise's value levers, with an estimate of what a consulting or advisory engagement could save. Deployed via GitHub and hosted on Cloudflare.
